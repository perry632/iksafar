Privacy Policy

How we protect your personal information

Home / Privacy Policy

Last Updated: January 2026

At Iksafar Travel, we are committed to protecting your privacy. This policy explains how we collect, use, and safeguard your personal information.

1. Information We Collect

We collect personal information to provide and improve our services:

  • Personal Details: Name, email address, phone number, date of birth, and address.
  • Travel Preferences: Destination, travel dates, package preferences, and special requests.
  • Payment Information: Credit/debit card details, UPI IDs, or bank account information (processed securely).
  • Usage Data: Information about how you use our website and services.

2. How We Use Your Information

We use your information for the following purposes:

  • To process and confirm your bookings.
  • To communicate with you about your travel plans.
  • To send you promotional offers and updates (you can opt out anytime).
  • To improve our services and customer experience.
  • To comply with legal and regulatory requirements.

3. Data Sharing & Security

We take data security seriously:

  • We do not sell or rent your personal information to third parties.
  • We share information only with trusted partners (e.g., hotels, transport providers) to fulfill your booking.
  • All data is stored on secure servers with encryption and firewalls.
  • We use secure payment gateways (Razorpay, Stripe, etc.) for transactions.
  • We regularly audit our security practices to protect your data.

4. Cookies & Tracking

We use cookies to enhance your experience:

  • Essential Cookies: Required for basic website functionality.
  • Analytics Cookies: Help us understand how visitors use our site.
  • Marketing Cookies: Used to show relevant ads and offers.
  • You can control cookie preferences in your browser settings.

5. Your Rights

You have the following rights regarding your data:

  • Access: Request a copy of your personal data.
  • Correction: Update or correct inaccurate information.
  • Deletion: Request deletion of your personal data.
  • Opt-out: Unsubscribe from marketing communications.
  • To exercise these rights, contact us at info@iksafar.com.

6. Third-Party Links

Our website may contain links to third-party websites:

  • We are not responsible for the privacy practices of these sites.
  • We encourage you to review their privacy policies.
  • We recommend caution when sharing personal information on third-party sites.

7. Children's Privacy

  • Our services are not directed at children under 13 years of age.
  • We do not knowingly collect personal information from children.
  • If you believe we have collected a child's data, please contact us to delete it.

8. Policy Updates

  • We may update this policy from time to time.
  • Significant changes will be notified via email or website notice.
  • We encourage you to review this policy periodically.

9. Contact Us
