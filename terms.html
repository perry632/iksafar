1. Booking & Payment

All bookings made with Iksafar Travel are subject to the following terms:

  • A non-refundable deposit of 25% is required at the time of booking.
  • Full payment must be made at least 15 days before the departure date.
  • Payments can be made via credit card, debit card, UPI, net banking, or bank transfer.
  • All prices are in Indian Rupees (INR) and are subject to change without prior notice.
  • We reserve the right to cancel bookings if payment is not received on time.

2. Cancellation & Refund

Our cancellation policy is designed to be fair and transparent:

  • Cancellation 30+ days before departure: Full refund (minus deposit).
  • Cancellation 15-30 days before departure: 50% refund.
  • Cancellation less than 15 days before departure: No refund.
  • Refunds will be processed within 7-10 business days.
  • All refunds are subject to bank processing fees and charges.
  • In case of force majeure (natural disasters, government restrictions, etc.), refunds will be processed as per the circumstances.

3. Travel Insurance

We strongly recommend that all travelers purchase comprehensive travel insurance:

  • Travel insurance is not included in the package price.
  • Insurance should cover trip cancellation, medical emergencies, and personal belongings.
  • We can recommend trusted insurance partners upon request.
  • We are not liable for any losses not covered by insurance.

5. Package Inclusions & Exclusions

Each package includes and excludes specific items. Please read carefully:

  • Inclusions: Accommodation, meals, guide services, and transportation as mentioned in the package.

We understand that plans can change. Here's our policy:
