Our Story

We Create Unforgettable Journeys

Founded in 2015, Iksafar Travel was born from a simple idea - to help travelers discover the authentic soul of India. What started as a small group of passionate travel enthusiasts has grown into a trusted travel company serving thousands of happy travelers.

Our name "Iksafar" combines "Ik" (meaning unique) and "Safar" (meaning journey) - representing our commitment to crafting unique travel experiences for every traveler.
